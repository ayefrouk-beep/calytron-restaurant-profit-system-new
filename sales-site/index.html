Where the money goes

A full dining room can still lose money.

Most owners watch sales. Profit is decided in the quiet places: what dishes really cost, what gets bought, what gets wasted and what the business spends.

Prices set by feel

If a dish was priced before ingredient costs changed, you may be selling a bestseller at a thin margin without knowing it.

Purchasing that drifts

Supplier prices move. Without purchases and stock in the same picture, rising costs hide inside a busy week.

Expenses nobody adds up

Small operating costs are easy to forget. Together they decide how much is actually left at month end.

Know the real cost of every dish, and decide with numbers, not hunches.
